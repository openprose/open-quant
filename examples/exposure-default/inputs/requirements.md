# Reporting requirements

- Account for J1–J6, both optimization records and their source identities. Preserve invalid proposals and missing joint evidence rather than clipping cells, dropping records or replacing absent data with another proposal.
- Distinguish formal sums on an invalid table from expectations under a probability law. Explain the conditioning of exposure on default, the marginal-product calculation and any equality or difference with joint loss. Independence and equality of one expectation are distinct findings.
- Apply an illustrative absolute arithmetic allowance of 1e-10 in each stated unit. Preserve USD-million scaling, horizon, probability basis and loss timing. The allowance is not an institutional acceptance limit.
- Keep reported aggregate agreement separate from verification using actual joint cells. With missing inputs, state which arithmetic relationships, common marginal calculations and bounds remain supported and which particular properties remain unresolved.
- Examine producer statements against the selected evidence. Explain the meaning and scope of the bounds, remaining evidence needs and limits on economic or institutional conclusions. No institutional facts are selected for completion or approval.
