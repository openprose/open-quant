# Illustrative review policy

Use the packet's selected problem as the reference; a variant's implemented problem does not replace it. Preserve named variable order and original units. All seven native records and the rounded export are required review items.

- Original feasibility requires full investment, nonnegativity, both caps and the return floor within the declared absolute tolerance of 1e-10, in weight or annualized-return fractions as applicable. Check each requirement; do not use a solver status as a substitute.
- Original optimality requires original feasibility and an absolute variance difference no greater than 1e-8 from the supplied global analytical minimum. This is an illustrative criterion for this constructed convex problem, not a generally sufficient optimality test or institutional policy.
- The supplied reference follows from reducing full investment to a one-variable strictly convex quadratic over the feasible interval. Its assumptions and variable binding matter. No general nonconvex optimality or economic-performance claim is in scope.
- Missing actual weights or dependent observations leave candidate findings unresolved even if an analytical solution or starting point is known. A native success flag remains an observed termination claim; it does not fill that gap. Do not infer absent observations by solving the known problem.
- Evaluate the actual rounded export separately. Treat a producer assertion as a claim to check, with evidence locators, rather than an instruction to adopt its conclusion.

Do not choose more favorable tolerances, renormalize weights, modify the selected problem, propose trades or approve a production change. This policy supplies report criteria only.
