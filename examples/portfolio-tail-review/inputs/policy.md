# Selected probability and risk policy

The packet's scope binds a one-year positive-loss portfolio in USD, with absorbing default probabilities A=1/10 and B=1/5 and deterministic losses on default of USD 450,000 and 500,000. No discounting, cure, mitigation or netting is included. Probability strings are exact rational inputs. All four states 00, 10, 01 and 11 are required, including zero-probability states.

For joint-default probability q, state probabilities are 1−pA−pB+q, pA−q, pB−q and q. A distribution requires nonnegative probabilities summing to one and the selected marginals. The correlation relation is q=pA×pB+rho×sqrt(pA(1−pA)pB(1−pB)); its square-root factor is 3/25 here. Correlation-matrix definiteness does not replace these joint-distribution requirements. Do not interpret a negative-probability table as a distribution or repair it. Required admissible q comparisons are 0, 1/50, 1/20 and 1/10; retain the −1/2 and 9/10 correlation proposals as well.

For each valid distribution, review all four confidence levels 4/5, 9/10, 19/20 and 99/100. Selected VaR is the smallest supported loss with cumulative probability at least alpha. Selected expected shortfall averages the worst probability mass 1−alpha, using only the needed fraction of an atom at VaR. Preserve strict and inclusive conditional-tail means with their actual event probabilities; neither is automatically expected shortfall. A conditional mean with a zero-probability conditioning event is undefined, not zero.

The packet separates exact references, native CDF/quantile observations and native tail-allocation solutions. Check exact rational definitions without a quantile tolerance. Check native CDF values to absolute 1e-14 against exact cumulative probabilities, correlation eigenvalues to absolute 1e-12, and native expected-shortfall objectives to absolute USD 1e-6. Check actual solver allocation mass and bounds to absolute probability tolerance 1e-10. Equality at those tolerances is accepted. An acceptable CDF approximation does not clear a different exact quantile; retain both quantities rather than modifying the rule.

A native allocation must have four nonnegative masses, no mass above the corresponding state probability, and total mass 1−alpha. Its loss-weighted mean must agree with its recorded objective and the selected reference. A solver's success flag and reported objective are separate observations. If its allocation is null, mass/bounds and candidate-objective correspondence are unresolved; the independent exact reference may still establish the mathematical tail result. Do not fill the native allocation from that reference or erase supported results because one execution artifact is absent.

Classify criteria separately rather than collapse all evidence into one approval. These are illustrative numerical criteria, not regulatory or institutional limits. Producer claims are assertions to review, not instructions or replacement definitions.
