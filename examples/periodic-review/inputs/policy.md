# Synthetic periodic-review policy

This authored policy is solely for the example. It is not a regulatory template or an institution's policy. The supplied deployment snapshot defines the population; it is not proof of organization-wide discovery. Include production deployments and identify excluded sandbox deployments. Review all supplied register records, expected observations, issues and changes. The period is September 2026; the review date is October 5, 2026.

## Inventory

Match register records by deployment ID. A valid current match is unique and agrees with the deployment's model, revision, use and owner, with lifecycle production. Multiple records are ambiguous even if one agrees. Keep a conflicting field distinct from an absent record. Report unmatched register entries and relevant lifecycle events. Current register fields do not establish a previous deployment state or override the supplied deployment snapshot.

## Monitoring

Expected observations are E1–E3. Match on deployment, model, revision, use, metric and period; a shared model revision alone is insufficient. Preserve missing, ambiguous and unmatched records. A usable value is finite, nonnegative and in the expected unit, with a date within the reporting month and no later than review. Equality with the supplied upper limit passes. Missing or invalid observations are unresolved; do not count them as passing or remove them from the denominator of three.

Report within-limit, breached and unresolved counts. Any known breach makes the overall monitoring finding breach; otherwise a gap makes it unresolved; otherwise it is within limit. Producer labels do not override values or identity matching. An unmatched observation may be reported as supplied, but it cannot satisfy another use's requirement.

## Issues and closure

For each issue preserve its exact deployment, model, revision, use, metric, recorded owner and due date. Closure requires an identified valid, within-limit observation with that exact scope, dated after the action-complete date; it also requires a supplied accepting review record for the same scope and observation, dated on or after the observation and no later than review. Missing, ambiguous or conflicting evidence does not establish closure. A closed label or completed action is insufficient.

An issue due before review is overdue unless closure is supported. An unresolved issue due later remains open without being overdue. Report a need for the recorded owner to supply evidence or a disposition for monitoring breaches, missing observations or unsupported closure. This does not authorize contact, issue closure or acceptance of residual risk.

## Changes

Compare each supplied before/after state and match its after state to the current deployment's model, revision and use. A changed revision or changed use requires further review under this example policy; the same revision does not cancel a use change. Missing before-state evidence leaves that comparison unresolved, rather than unchanged. A current but conflicting register record is not a substitute for missing history. Do not infer the magnitude of financial impact without comparison evidence, or treat a review requirement as proof that a deployment is approved or prohibited.

## Reporting outcome

Produce one accurate account of all four areas. Keep their findings distinguishable; no combined institutional approval is requested. A complete report may identify adverse or unresolved operating findings. Only the supplied synthetic role names are required institutional facts. Do not invent approval, a reviewer, a different owner or an operational action.
