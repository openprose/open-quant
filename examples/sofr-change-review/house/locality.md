# Additional locality requirement

This is an illustrative additional house requirement, not a universal definition of a good curve.

For the supplied +1 bp perturbation of the 5Y input quote, the maximum forward change outside the 4Y–6Y window must be at most **1 bp**. This requirement applies in addition to the selected shape requirements; satisfying one set does not waive the other.

The window uses the supplied calculation's 4Y and 6Y instrument pillar dates: **2030-09-25 through 2032-09-24**, inclusive inside the window. It is not the interval between exact Act/365F times 4 and 6. Use the supplied off-window measure for each method. An in-window response, a change under another perturbation, or an explanation that the tradeoff is acceptable does not establish this numerical requirement. A proposed change to the limit is a new agreement, not fulfillment of this one.
