# Review option quote fit and joint consistency

**For agents.** Execute under the caller's selected OpenProse kernel. The working root is the source repository, two directories above this program.

Adopt [calibration review](../../contracts/calibration-review.md) and [implementation review](../../contracts/implementation-review.md), including their adopted definitions, for one report. Apply [the brief](inputs/brief.md) and [review policy](inputs/policy.md). The caller selects `complete`, `missing-underlying` or `contradictory`; ask for an absent or unknown selection. Read only its [complete](inputs/complete.json), [missing-underlying](inputs/missing-underlying.json) or [contradictory](inputs/contradictory.json) packet and [receipt](receipt.json).

Produce a report under 1,100 words excluding identity locators for an AI-literate financial operations reader. Account for all ten quote inversions, twelve strategies and 36 spread-dependent costs. Distinguish individual fit, joint price constraints, mathematical payoff under the calculation assumptions, and evidence supporting those assumptions for the selected instruments. Preserve known findings alongside unresolved evidence. Review any producer assertions against the records.

Write only `report.md` and `result.md` in a fresh directory under `results/option-consistency/`. Identify the case, program, adopted definitions, brief, policy, selected packet and receipt by computed hash. Attribute calculation-source identity to the receipt rather than claiming to have inspected or reproduced it. State checks performed, reporting fulfillment, remaining work and the actual output path.

Use only those sources and definitions. Arithmetic and identity checks are permitted. Do not inspect reproduction source, other cases, reference reports or test answers to fill withheld instrument terms. Do not rerun the model, fetch market records, repair quotes or undertake financial action. Reading restrictions are instructions, not enforced filesystem isolation. A report can fulfill this agreement while finding inconsistent prices or unresolved applicability.
