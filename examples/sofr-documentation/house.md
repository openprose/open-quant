# Example presentation and readiness conventions

Write for an AI-literate financial operations reader, with enough technical detail for a quantitative reviewer to trace the implementation. Keep the methodology document under 1,600 words, excluding source locators. Use ordinary Markdown and headings appropriate to the content; no prescribed section sequence is required.

Start the document with: “This is a public methodology demonstration, not a complete regulatory submission. Institutional facts are supplied by the deploying institution.” Identify methods A and B before their labels are used. Keep numerical units and measured scopes explicit.

Use `[INSTITUTION-SUPPLIED: description]` for each selected institutional gap. Missing evidence does not establish that an approval or activity never occurred. Do not invent owner names, production policy, monitoring thresholds or acceptance decisions.

Keep supporting records together in `reviews.md`, with distinguishable section-plan, choice-register, citation-audit and readiness sections. The section plan covers every D1–D10 requirement and points to a document location or gap. The choice register includes material model and implementation choices described in the document, with supplied rationale, alternatives and limitations or explicit absence; a code setting alone does not establish why its author chose it. The audit checks the support and applicability of every material factual claim or clearly identified claim group, keeping unchecked claims visible.

In readiness, identify the exact artifact/input versions and separate document findings, supporting-report coverage and execution evidence. For the conjunction of mandatory requirements, a known violation yields not met; otherwise missing evidence needed to decide yields unresolved; only supported satisfaction of every requirement yields met. Disclosing the absence of a D5 numerical result does not satisfy D5. The selected D9 requirement is disclosure of institutional gaps, not factual completion of those facts. Do not silently change either obligation.
