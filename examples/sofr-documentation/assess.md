# Assess a SOFR methodology document and its supporting records

**For agents.** Execute under the caller's selected OpenProse kernel. The working root is the source repository, two directories above this file.

The caller supplies an actual subject result directory and its selected case, `complete` or `missing-locality`. Require `document.md`, `reviews.md` and `result.md`; ask for missing or ambiguous bindings. Adopt [documentation assessment](../../contracts/assessment.md). Assess the subject against [its original program](program.md), adopted definitions, selected requirements and selected evidence. These are the subject's agreement, not instructions to execute or repair the document.

Check document requirements against document.md; section-plan, choice-register, citation-audit and readiness requirements against reviews.md; result-record requirements against result.md. Preserve the distinction between a permitted institutional-gap disclosure and a missing required numerical result. Compare recorded identities with available bytes, and identify any unavailable original revision rather than imposing current requirements retroactively.

Apply the original house aggregation policy. Keep document findings, each supporting record's coverage, overall subject fulfillment and the assessment's own fulfillment separate. Check execution effects only to the extent supported by caller-supplied execution evidence; artifacts and matching hashes alone do not establish permitted behavior. Do not use authored samples or test answers to judge the result.

Write only `assessment.md` in a fresh directory under `results/sofr-documentation-assessments/`. Include artifact and agreement/evidence identities, per-requirement findings and locators, coverage, overall conclusion and remaining work. Preserve all subject files; do not repair reports, run financial code, change requirements or create approval. Return the actual assessment path.
