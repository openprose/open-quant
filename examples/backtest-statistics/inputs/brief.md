# Exception-statistics review

Review six deliberately constructed sequences of 250 binary observations. One means an exception; zero means no exception. Positions are one-based and the sequence is linear. The identifiers BT01 through BT06 are labels, not evidence about their patterns. All probabilities are fractions unless explicitly converted to percentages.

The supplied aggregate counts are the selected inputs for frequency calculations. Ordered indicators and exception positions, where available, support recounting and verification of timing statistics. Native quantities and separate arithmetic references are calculation evidence; a reported statistic alone does not establish that its underlying sequence supports it.

This scope begins with synthetic exception indicators. Actual forecasts, losses, forecast availability, observation dates and forecast/outcome pairing are not supplied. Identify those boundaries rather than inventing a completed production or regulatory backtest. The requested output is an evidence review, not approval of a financial model or a recommendation to change capital or positions.
