# Historical input review policy

For each requested decision, eligible input records must match its entity and observation date exactly. Both publication and known local availability must be at or before the UTC cutoff; equality is allowed. Select the highest eligible revision. Conflicting records at that revision remain ambiguous; record ID is not authorized precedence. Publication, availability, represented date and revision order are distinct fields.

Review observed selections against the supplied source records independently. A selector name, intended method, producer assertion or recorded selection alone does not provide missing timestamp evidence. If local availability is unknown, report the resulting limitation without asserting that the record was necessarily late or that no execution occurred. A known wrong period, future timestamp, stale revision or unresolved conflict remains a finding even if another fact is missing.

For a uniquely selected eligible candidate, explain the supporting identity and timing. Distinguish a selection known to breach policy, no eligible record in the supplied history, a retained conflict, and an unresolved timing claim. An absent numeric prediction for an unavailable or ambiguous input is not a zero-error result. Do not fill, backdate, deduplicate or revise the supplied records.

Reconstruct each reported absolute-error sum and denominator from its observed predictions and outcomes. Also compare the fixed common IDs D02, D04, D05, D06, D07 and D10. That common population is a declared arithmetic comparison, not a certification that every selector's inputs are eligible. Preserve the ten requested decisions and all exclusions; do not rank models from incompatible populations or infer economic usefulness from this constructed score.

No statistical significance, production readiness or institutional approval threshold is supplied. Report supported findings and unresolved requirements, not an invented all-clear decision.
