# Presentation and assessment conventions

Use ordinary Markdown. Keep document.md under 1,500 words excluding source locators. Define terms for an AI-literate financial operations reader and retain enough detail for a quantitative reviewer to trace the source. No section sequence is prescribed.

Identify the document as a public synthetic-methodology example, with institutional records absent. Use `[INSTITUTION-SUPPLIED: description]` for each missing fact. Do not turn a missing public mathematical result into an institutional placeholder.

Keep the section plan, choice register, citation audit and readiness report together in reviews.md, with each distinguishable. Map every D1–D8 requirement to a document location or gap, and I1–I3 when selected. For each material choice, identify the selected convention, supplied reason, alternative, limitation and evidence, marking absent rationale. Audit each material factual claim or clearly identified claim group for support and applicability; list unfinished checks explicitly.

In readiness, distinguish document requirements, supporting-record coverage and execution evidence. For mandatory requirements, a supported violation yields not met; otherwise unavailable evidence needed to decide yields unresolved; only support for every mandatory requirement yields met. An absent required institutional entry in the document is a known content shortfall under I1–I3, even though the underlying real-world fact remains unknown. Matching hashes and complete-looking artifacts alone do not establish execution behavior.

Keep the assessment's own coverage and fulfillment separate from the subject's conclusion. No comprehensive institutional readiness, regulatory acceptance or approval follows from this example's selected aggregation policy.
