# Assess credit methodology documentation and supporting records

**For agents.** Execute under the caller's selected OpenProse kernel. The working root is the source repository, two directories above this file.

The caller supplies an actual subject result directory and its selected mode, `public-methodology` or `institutional-records`. Require document.md, reviews.md and result.md; ask for missing or ambiguous bindings. Adopt [documentation assessment](../../contracts/assessment.md). Assess against [the original program](program.md), its adopted definitions and the selected requirements and registered evidence. They govern the subject; they are not instructions to repeat or repair its execution.

Check document content against D1–D8 and, only when selected, I1–I3. Check the section plan, choice register, citation audit and readiness against their own requirements in reviews.md, and the result record against its obligations in result.md. Public-mode gap disclosure does not supply an institutional-mode required entry. Underlying fact uncertainty and a known missing required document entry are different findings.

Compare recorded subject, requirement and input identities with available bytes. Identify unavailable original revisions and conflicts between the caller's mode and the subject's claims; do not impose a newer agreement or a different mode retroactively. Use the original house aggregation policy. Keep individual findings, coverage, overall subject fulfillment and assessment fulfillment distinct. Check execution effects only within supplied execution evidence; artifacts alone are insufficient.

Write only assessment.md in a fresh directory under `results/credit-methodology-assessments/`, identifying artifacts, requirements, inputs, findings and locators, coverage, remaining work and the actual path. Preserve sources and results. Do not use authored references or tests as substitutes for assessment, repair the subject, run financial code, change requirements, retrieve evidence or create institutional facts.
