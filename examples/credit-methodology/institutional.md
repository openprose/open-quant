# Supply the selected institutional records

**For agents.** Additional requirements only when adopted by the `institutional-records` mode. These are authored example requirements, not a statement of regulatory obligations.

| ID | Additional required content in the methodology document |
|---|---|
| I1 | Identify an accountable model owner, the relevant organization and the effective scope/date, supported by an institution-supplied ownership record. |
| I2 | Identify the actual deployed model revision, approved use and applicable restrictions, supported by the corresponding deployment/use-decision records. Distinguish intended use from observed deployment. |
| I3 | Identify the reviewer, reviewed revision, scope, dated decision and outstanding conditions, supported by the corresponding review record. Do not claim independent review without evidence for that claim. |

Required but unavailable entries remain missing required content. Reporting their absence does not satisfy I1–I3, although it may satisfy the separate obligation to disclose gaps. Unknown facts must not be fabricated. A correct account of missing records is useful partial work; it is not fulfillment of this additional agreement or evidence that the institution never performed these activities.

These entries document selected records. They do not grant approval, authorize deployment, establish model soundness or exhaust an institution's requirements.
