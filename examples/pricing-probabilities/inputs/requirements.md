# Reporting requirements

- Account for all six payoffs and both named worlds. Identify available and unavailable evidence without treating absent quantities as zero or copying assumptions from another world.
- Distinguish present price from terminal expected payoff and identify the probability measure used for each expectation. Keep state-price normalization and discounting explicit. Illustrative arithmetic tolerance is 1e-10 in each quantity's stated unit; it is not an institutional acceptance limit.
- Explain whether replication and consistent prices identify physical event probabilities. Distinguish an implication of stipulated assumptions from an empirically supported forecast. Preserve the equality control so the report does not claim P and Q must always differ.
- Examine any supplied producer statements against the permitted evidence. Identify material contradictions and supported limitations; do not infer that adverse underlying findings prevent fulfillment of an accurate reporting obligation.
- State the evidence needed for unresolved intended-use conclusions and the limits of this two-state construction. No organizational facts are selected for completion or approval.
