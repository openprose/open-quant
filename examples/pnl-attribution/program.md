# Review a full-revaluation P&L explanation

**For agents.** Execute under the caller's selected OpenProse kernel. The working root is the source repository, two directories above this program.

Adopt [P&L explanation](../../contracts/pnl-explanation.md) and [sensitivity review](../../contracts/sensitivity-review.md), including their adopted definitions, for one report. Apply [the brief](inputs/brief.md) and [review policy](inputs/policy.md). The caller selects `complete`, `missing-corner` or `contradictory`; ask for an absent or unknown selection. Read only its [complete](inputs/complete.json), [missing-corner](inputs/missing-corner.json) or [contradictory](inputs/contradictory.json) packet and [receipt](receipt.json).

Produce a report under 1,100 words excluding identity locators for an AI-literate financial operations reader. Account for all eight corner records, six sequential paths, standalone effects and their residual, the selected three-factor allocation and the two-group comparison. Distinguish arithmetic reconciliation, source support, the selected method and an economic explanation. Preserve known findings alongside unresolved inputs, and review producer assertions against the records.

Write only `report.md` and `result.md` in a fresh directory under `results/pnl-attribution/`. Identify the case, program, adopted definitions, brief, policy, selected packet and receipt by computed hash. Attribute calculation-source identity to the receipt rather than claiming to have inspected or reproduced it. State checks performed, reporting fulfillment, remaining work and the actual output path.

Use only those sources and definitions. Arithmetic and identity checks are permitted. Do not inspect reproduction source, other cases, reference reports or test answers to fill missing records. Do not rerun pricing, fetch market records, invent a market narrative or post an accounting entry. Reading restrictions are instructions, not enforced filesystem isolation. Accurate reporting can fulfill this agreement while identifying incomplete support for an allocation.
