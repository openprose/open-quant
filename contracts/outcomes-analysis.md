# Review model predictions against outcomes

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md).

The caller supplies the model revision and use, prediction and outcome records, pairing rules, evaluation period and population, metric definitions, available benchmark and uncertainty evidence, and acceptance criteria. A benchmark is required only if the caller asks for a comparative conclusion.

Relate each prediction to its corresponding outcome under the supplied horizon and timing rules. Account for unavailable, immature, excluded and unmatched outcomes. Identify whether predictions were produced before the outcomes became available; describe supplied evidence of training or selection overlap that limits an out-of-sample claim.

Report performance with its population, denominator, units, weighting and definition. Preserve uncertainty, relevant segments and time variation where the supplied evidence supports them. Distinguish outcome error, calibration, discrimination and economic usefulness rather than treating one metric as proof of all four. A comparison requires compatible populations and evaluation conditions.

Identify limitations of the sample and any test-selection or tuning history supplied by the caller. Apply the stated acceptance criteria without inventing significance or treating an unavailable outcome as a favorable result. This report does not establish performance in unobserved regimes or authorize a model's continued use.
