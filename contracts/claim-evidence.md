# Support documentation claims

## Inputs

The caller supplies the document scope, permitted evidence and any required evidence relationship or revision policy. Apply this contract to the caller's specified document or report; do not assume every file in the environment is a permitted source.

## Requirements

Support each material factual claim with an accessible locator in the supplied evidence. A shared table locator is sufficient when it makes each mapping unambiguous; otherwise provide a more specific locator. Preserve the subject, method, quantity, units, date and population to which the evidence applies.

Check the asserted relationship, not merely whether its number occurs somewhere in the sources. A correct count can support a false ranking. A difference between methods is not automatically a gain, loss, profit or evidence that one is correct.

Distinguish numerical observations from a developer's stated rationale and external claims. Attribute the rationale to the brief. Do not treat an external source title, inaccessible paper or unsupported reference as evidence of its contents.

Report conflicting, missing or inapplicable evidence. Do not invent support or silently choose the more convenient source. Evidence from another result or revision requires the relationship permitted by the selected agreement; neither equal nor different file hashes alone establish semantic support.

State what checks establish and leave other claims unresolved. Exact arithmetic and byte identities are evidence within their scope, not certification of the model or the full document.
