# Review quantitative scenario results

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md).

The caller supplies the model and portfolio revisions, base case, scenario definitions, horizons, assumptions, results and acceptance criteria. Identify any intended management actions or balance-sheet changes and whether the results include them.

Explain each scenario's changes and the resulting effects relative to the compatible base case. Preserve currency, horizon, exposure population, valuation basis, dependence assumptions and scenario identity. Distinguish historical, hypothetical and reverse-stress constructions where that information is supplied. Do not assign scenario probabilities or expected losses without a supported probability model.

Account for all requested scenarios and affected outputs, including failed runs and omitted exposures. Explain aggregation and possible double counting where supplied results overlap. Keep unmitigated effects separate from effects after assumed actions, and distinguish a modeled action from an authorized or feasible action.

Report vulnerabilities and limits supported by the scenarios. The worst observed result is worst among the tested scenarios, not necessarily the worst possible result. Passing supplied scenario limits does not establish complete risk coverage or institutional approval.
