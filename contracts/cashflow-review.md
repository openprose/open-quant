# Review instrument cash-flow evidence

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md).

The caller supplies the instruments or legs, contractual terms and revisions, schedules or calculation records, required review scope, relevant dates and acceptance criteria. Identify the expected flow population, currency, notional, payment direction and units. Preserve coupon, principal, fee and other flow identities; distinct obligations on the same date are not automatically duplicates.

Review the supplied flows against the selected terms. Keep accrual boundaries, day-count conventions, unadjusted dates, payment-date adjustments and calendars distinct. Preserve relevant schedule rules, rates or fixing evidence and principal changes where the selected instruments require them. Missing terms or observations leave the affected conclusion unresolved; do not supply market conventions from an instrument label alone.

Account for each required flow, its amount, date, calculation basis and supporting evidence. Distinguish an independently observed reference from an assertion of correctness or another value derived from the same inputs. Agreement with arithmetic under different conventions does not establish compliance with the selected terms. A close aggregate amount or valuation cannot clear a known flow-level discrepancy.

When reviewing a valuation, identify the settlement and discount reference dates, treatment of flows on those dates, discounting basis and included population. Keep past, included and excluded flows visible with their reasons. A change in inclusion can change the comparison without changing a coupon amount. Do not conflate the coupon schedule, accrued interest, clean or dirty price, and absolute present value.

Report supported matches, discrepancies and evidence gaps within the requested scope. Distinguish projected or contractual flows from payment instructions, confirmations and reconciled receipts. A generated schedule or valuation does not establish entitlement, actual payment or ledger agreement. This review does not authorize changing terms, generating missing observations, booking adjustments or transferring funds.
