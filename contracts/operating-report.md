# Report on quantitative operating work

**For agents.** Common requirements for the operating contracts that explicitly adopt this definition.

## Bindings

The caller supplies the reporting question, subject identities and revisions, permitted evidence, applicable house policy, reporting date or period, intended reader and output location. Identify missing bindings that prevent a conclusion. Do not create an institution's policy from an illustrative threshold or a public source.

Adopt [claim evidence](claim-evidence.md) and [institutional facts](institutional-facts.md). Apply their requirements to this report. The caller identifies the institutional facts relevant to the selected obligation.

## Required result

Answer the reporting question for the supplied scope. Distinguish observations, conclusions under the supplied policy, unresolved evidence and proposed actions. Cite the source records that support each material finding, including contradictory records. Identify any calculation or transformation you performed and its inputs; a calculation does not supply missing observations.

Account for the caller's expected population and period, including missing, duplicate, excluded and unmatched records. Preserve record identity; do not silently merge ambiguous records. If the population itself is unavailable, limit claims of completeness accordingly. Distinguish no observed exception from evidence that all required items were checked.

State whether this reporting obligation was fulfilled, which required work remains, and what the findings establish about the subject. A complete report can identify a failed control, an unresolved fact or a model limitation. It does not establish that the underlying issue is resolved. Conversely, favorable observed values do not establish that the report covered the required scope.

## Boundary

Use permitted evidence and tools; write only to the supplied result location and preserve earlier results. Do not change source data, models, production configuration, thresholds or institutional records. Model execution, external retrieval, publication, notification and operational action require explicit authority. A proposed action is not an action taken.
