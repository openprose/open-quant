# Explain a supplied profit-and-loss movement

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md).

The caller supplies the portfolio and model revisions, opening and closing dates, P&L definition, total movement, explanatory components and their derivation, treatment of trades, cash flows, fees and adjustments, and reconciliation tolerance.

Reconcile the supplied movement to its explanatory components under that definition. Preserve sign, currency, valuation basis, population and time interval. Identify excluded or overlapping components and keep the unexplained residual visible; do not distribute it across factors to make the numbers agree.

Separate exact revaluation from approximation-based explanations and report interaction or higher-order terms where supplied. A numerical reconciliation establishes arithmetic consistency, not the causal correctness of factor labels. Explain missing support and competing explanations rather than inventing market narratives.

Distinguish economic P&L, accounting P&L, hypothetical P&L and risk-theoretical P&L according to the caller's definitions. A daily explanatory bridge is not automatically the regulatory P&L attribution test. Do not book trades, revise marks or post accounting entries.
