# Review model data and lineage

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md).

The caller supplies the model use, required input population and period, source and transformation evidence, model-input snapshot, and applicable quality and freshness criteria. Identify absent lineage rather than reconstructing an unsupported history.

Describe how the supplied source data relates to the selected model inputs, retaining identifiers, dates, units, conventions and relevant transformations. Distinguish observed transformation evidence from documented intended processing. Identify proxies, overrides, exclusions and manual adjustments with their supplied reasons and authority.

Report missing, duplicate, stale or inconsistent data against the supplied criteria, preserving the affected population and denominator. Distinguish missing values from zeros, legitimate repeated observations from ambiguous duplicates, and publication time from the date represented by a value.

Explain how identified gaps limit the particular model use or the ability to assess it. A technically well-formed dataset is not proof of relevance or representativeness. Do not fill values, change a feed or repair a production dataset as part of this review.
