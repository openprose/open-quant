# Review credit-loss model evidence

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md), including their adopted definitions.

The caller supplies the selected credit-loss method and use, model/input revisions, exposure population, default definition, probability and loss assumptions, observed calculations and acceptance criteria. Identify the currency, valuation date, horizons and probability basis. Distinguish stipulated, empirically estimated and market-implied probabilities; one does not establish another.

Explain whether each probability is cumulative, an unconditional interval probability or conditional on survival or another event. Preserve that conditioning when relating probabilities across horizons and to the loss calculation. Distinguish hazard rates from probabilities. Check the supplied evidence for the relationships required by the selected method; individually bounded values do not establish a consistent or correctly applied default distribution.

Bind exposure and loss severity to their stated meaning on default, including applicable recovery, collateral or netting assumptions. Distinguish loss fractions from recovery fractions and loss amounts. When the method factors a loss expectation or combines scenarios, identify its conditioning, dependence and weighting assumptions; do not infer that products of marginal means equal an expected loss. Preserve loss and recovery timing, discounting, units and covered exposures in component and aggregate comparisons.

Report met, breached and unresolved criteria separately. Missing default, exposure or loss evidence is not a zero loss. Mathematical consistency and implementation agreement do not establish calibration, appropriate accounting treatment, economic suitability or institutional approval. Reviewing supplied calculations does not authorize recalibration, a credit decision, a valuation adjustment or an accounting entry unless separately included in the caller's scope.
