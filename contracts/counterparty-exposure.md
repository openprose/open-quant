# Review counterparty exposure evidence

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md), including their adopted definitions.

The caller supplies the selected exposure measure and use, valuation date and horizons, model and input revisions, trade population, counterparty and netting-set mappings, collateral records, recognition rules, calculation evidence and acceptance criteria. Define the measure's sign, currency, aggregation and any scenario weights or quantile convention. Distinguish current exposure, expected exposure, potential future exposure and exposure at default; agreement in one measure does not establish another.

Account for trades within their supplied netting sets and counterparties. Identify the evidence supporting the applicable netting recognition. Shared counterparty identity, a database label or offsetting values do not establish permission to net across sets. Distinguish stipulated assumptions from documented institutional recognition and unresolved facts. Missing membership or recognition evidence is not a zero contribution or authority to pool positions; identify any calculation that remains conditional on it.

Relate recognized collateral to its assigned exposures, valuation time, currency and conversion direction, received or posted status, settlement, eligibility and applicable adjustments. Preserve supplied allocation, segregation and reuse restrictions. A recorded balance, pending transfer or proposed allocation does not by itself establish an available recognized offset. Account for shared allocations without duplicating recognized value; do not infer authority to transfer surplus between sets.

Reconcile component and aggregate results under the selected order of conversion, adjustment, netting and flooring. Preserve record multiplicities and explain differences caused by scope or conventions. Correct sums or agreement with a reference implementation do not establish that the selected exposure definition and recognition rules were followed.

Report supported findings, breaches and unresolved evidence separately. This review does not establish legal enforceability, regulatory eligibility or institutional approval, and does not authorize trades, collateral movements, margin calls or changes to exposure records.
