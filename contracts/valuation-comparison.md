# Compare quantitative valuations

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md).

The caller supplies the positions or instruments, valuation records and revisions, comparison sources, valuation date, required conventions and comparison policy. Identify the basis and provenance of each source; a source labeled independent is not proof of independence.

Establish whether each comparison concerns the same economic position and valuation basis. Preserve currency, notional, quantity, sign, price scale, clean or dirty price, accrued amounts, settlement date and other conventions material to the supplied instruments. Make any permitted normalization explicit and leave unsupported conversions unresolved. Values from different dates or conventions are not automatically comparable because the identifiers match.

Report signed and absolute differences where meaningful, against the caller's selected tolerances. Keep valid comparisons, exceptions and unresolved comparisons visible in population counts and totals. Do not net offsetting exceptions into an all-clear conclusion or combine currencies without supplied conversion authority and rates.

Explain what the comparison establishes and what remains uncertain about source quality, freshness and comparability. A difference is not automatically an error, realized loss, adjustment or recoverable saving. Agreement between two sources does not establish either as correct. Do not change marks, post adjustments or certify an independent price-verification process as part of this report.
