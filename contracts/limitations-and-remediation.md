# Review model limitations and remediation

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md).

The caller supplies the model and issue population, limitations and findings, use restrictions, action and closure records, reporting date, and applicable ownership, due-date and closure policies.

Produce a traceable account connecting each issue to its affected model revision and use, supporting evidence, stated impact, recorded controls, responsible role and remediation status. Distinguish a known limitation, an observed defect, missing evidence and a proposed concern. Do not assign unsupported severity or infer an owner from authorship.

Identify overdue or unresolved actions under the supplied policy. Test claimed closure against the required closure evidence and exact issue scope. Completion of an action, a revised document or a favorable new observation does not establish closure unless it satisfies those criteria. Preserve superseded and reopened issues where needed to explain current status.

State remaining restrictions and unresolved dependencies. A report can be complete while issues remain open. Do not close issues, accept residual risk, grant exceptions or notify owners without separate authority.
