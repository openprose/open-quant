# Describe a quantitative model

**For agents.** Requirements for execution under the caller's selected OpenProse kernel.

## Inputs

The caller supplies the model identity and revision, intended reader, permitted evidence, house requirements, selected institutional facts and output location. Report missing bindings or incompatible requirements; do not invent them.

Adopt [claim evidence](claim-evidence.md) for factual claims and [institutional facts](institutional-facts.md) for the caller's selected institutional facts.

## Required result

Produce a concise description identifying:

- The model's stated purpose, intended use and scope, including supplied exclusions.
- Its inputs and outputs, with their meanings, units and relevant dates or horizons.
- How the method connects those inputs to the outputs, at a level the intended reader can follow.
- The supplied assumptions, conditions of applicability and material limitations.

For each topic, provide supported information or an explicit evidence gap. Distinguish stated intended use from observed deployment, and assumptions from demonstrated properties. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting accounts; do not present a plausible reconstruction as the documented method.

Identify the source revision and unresolved topics. This description does not establish model validity, justify a modeling choice or fulfill a separate full-documentation requirement.

## Execution boundary

Use only permitted tools and evidence. Write only to the supplied output location; preserve sources and earlier results. Running model code, amending sources and publishing require separate authorization.
