# Describe the impact of a model change

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md) and [numerical evidence](numerical-evidence.md) for supplied numerical comparisons.

The caller identifies the previous and proposed revisions, intended change, affected uses and dependencies, available comparison evidence, and the policy for materiality and further review. Missing previous-state evidence limits comparison; it does not make the proposal an unchanged model.

Describe what changed in method, implementation, parameters, data or use, to the extent supported by evidence. Separate an intended change from the observed differences. Relate each material finding to the affected outputs, documentation, controls or downstream uses in the supplied scope.

Preserve comparison conditions, including market date, population and input changes. Identify confounded comparisons and unexplained differences. Do not attribute a difference to the code change when data, configuration or scope also changed without sufficient evidence to separate their effects.

Apply the supplied materiality policy, report required follow-up and distinguish proposed actions from recorded approvals. A small observed difference does not establish universal equivalence. A change-impact report does not approve deployment, modify a model or complete an independent validation.
