# Review evidence for a vendor model

**For agents.** Adopt [operating report](operating-report.md).

The caller supplies the vendor product and version, intended local use, available vendor material and local testing, customization records, applicable review requirements and permitted evidence. Distinguish the vendor's published product from the institution's configured implementation.

Report what is known about purpose, methods, inputs, assumptions, limitations and performance, attributing vendor claims separately from locally observed evidence. Identify unavailable proprietary information and the resulting limits on conclusions. Unavailable internals do not prove either soundness or unsoundness.

Explain supplied local customizations, overlays and dependencies and whether the evidence addresses that particular version, configuration and use. A generic vendor validation statement does not establish the institution's local implementation meets its requirements. Identify conflicting documents, untested upgrades and continuing evidence obligations under the caller's policy.

State supported findings, unanswered questions and required local decisions. Do not invent contractual access rights, approvals, vendor responses or compensating controls. This report does not procure a product, contact the vendor or independently validate undisclosed internals.
